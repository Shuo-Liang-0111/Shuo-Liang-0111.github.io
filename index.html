RA-Bench

Can We Defend Against AI-Generated Video Attacks on Real-World Crisis Events? A Systematic Evaluation of Detectors, Generators and Social Dissemination

Shuo Liang*, Yixing Ma*, Pengfei Zhou*, …, Chunhua Shen, Wei Wang, Yang You, Zheng Zhu, Kaipeng Zhang, Wangbo Zhao

Co-first author · listed first #1 on Hugging Face Daily Papers

RA-Bench pairs 1,830 real crisis videos with 16,056 generated counterparts from nine generators, then adds a human-deception stress set and a six-stage social-dissemination simulation. I designed the evaluation protocol, built the pipelines, ran every experiment, and wrote the paper; the released dataset contains 17,886 clips (93.8 GB).

DFPR

Dynamic Fine-Grained Perceptive Bidirectional Image-Text Retrieval

Mengru Ma, Shuo Liang, Wenping Ma, Lingling Li, Xu Liu, Xu Cong

First student author

DFPR is a parameter-efficient framework for remote-sensing image–text retrieval. It updates 6.79M parameters (4.49% of full fine-tuning) while improving mean recall over HarMA by 3.21, 1.87, and 2.94 points on RSITMD, RSICD, and UCM-Captions. As the first student author, I worked across the full research cycle—from problem and method formulation to implementation, experiments, rebuttal, and paper writing.

Research questions

World models should not get credit for looking plausible while quietly breaking the world. I am interested in models whose predictions remain geometrically grounded, temporally consistent, and useful for action.

01

Geometry as a constraint

Can depth, disparity, camera motion, and multi-view cues make generated futures preserve 3D structure across viewpoint changes?

02

Consistency over long rollouts

How can a model retain object identity, spatial relationships, and causal state instead of drifting into a different world?

03

Failures as learning signals

Can localized, human-aligned evaluation signals become rewards, curricula, or self-evaluation loops that improve generation and planning?

RA-Bench gives me the measurement side of this agenda; DFPR taught me to build and stress-test parameter-efficient vision-language systems. The next step I want to take is training geometry-aware world models, not only scoring their outputs after the fact.
