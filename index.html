RA-Bench

Can We Defend Against AI-Generated Video Attacks on Real-World Crisis Events? A Systematic Evaluation of Detectors, Generators and Social Dissemination

Shuo Liang*, Yixing Ma*, Pengfei Zhou*, …, Chunhua Shen, Wei Wang, Yang You, Zheng Zhu, Kaipeng Zhang, Wangbo Zhao

Co-first author · listed first #1 on Hugging Face Daily Papers

RA-Bench pairs 1,830 real crisis videos with 16,056 clips from nine video generators (17,886 clips, 93.8 GB), alongside a 633-clip human-deception stress set and a six-stage social-dissemination simulation. Across conventional detectors, zero-shot multimodal models, and fine-tuned MLLMs, no detector family generalizes reliably across generators; clips that deceive human viewers tend to fool detectors as well, and routine social-media compression and re-sharing further degrade detection.

DFPR

Dynamic Fine-Grained Perceptive Bidirectional Image-Text Retrieval

Mengru Ma, Shuo Liang, Wenping Ma, Lingling Li, Xu Liu, Cong Xu

First student author

DFPR is a parameter-efficient CLIP framework for remote-sensing image–text retrieval that combines sparse MoE routing, spectral graph filtering, Grounded-SAM priors, and bidirectional calibration. Updating 6.79M parameters (4.49% of full fine-tuning), it improves mean recall over HarMA by 3.21, 1.87, and 2.94 points on RSITMD, RSICD, and UCM-Captions.

Research focus

Visual plausibility is not enough when an agent has to act in a 3D world. I am interested in geometry-grounded world models and self-improving feedback loops that turn evaluation failures into better models and strategies.

01

3D geometry in world models

Using depth, camera motion, and multi-view constraints so predicted futures preserve coherent 3D structure across viewpoint changes.

02

Long-horizon state consistency

Preventing spatial drift over long rollouts so object identity, layout, and causal state stay reliable for embodied planning.

03

Recursive self-improvement (RSI)

Closing the loop from evaluation to training so localized failure signals revise an agent's world model, data curriculum, or learning strategy.

RA-Bench and DFPR grounded my work in evaluation and parameter-efficient multimodal modeling; ARC-AGI-3D brings that into interactive 3D tasks. Next, I want to train world models and agents that use feedback directly rather than relying on post-hoc scoring.
